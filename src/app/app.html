Next-Gen Power Backup

Halo Home UPS

Hykon Halo Inverter, with its inbuilt Lithium Ferro-Phosphate (LiFePO4) offers a modern alternative to traditional tubular batteries, ensuring a healthier, fumes-free environment for both homes and offices.

Halo can also be used as portable power pack in case of an emergency. Available in 2 variants; 1200 VA & 3000 VA, Halo inverters are the future of power backup with its modern design and advanced technology.
